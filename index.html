Hermes Personal

Hermes Personal is a private, single-user AI assistant operated by its owner for personal productivity. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Reads and writes the owner's personal notes stored in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Reads and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar when the owner asks.
  • Reads the owner's Gmail and sends email on the owner's behalf only when the owner explicitly asks.
  • Reads and updates the owner's own Google Sheets (personal budgeting).

Who can use it

Only the owner's own Google accounts sign in to this app. No other person's data is accessed.
